## 3. Category conventions (Slovak designers of RD, from the 50-company research)

1. Hero = a photorealistic render of a white cube house at sunset.
2. Beige / grey / white palette, thin sans, "moderné, nadčasové, kvalitné".
3. "Služby" grid: štúdia, projekt, inžiniering, dozor – icons in circles.
4. No prices ("cenová ponuka na mieru").
5. Certifications as a sentence ("autorizovaný inžinier"), no number.
6. Energy as an adjective ("nízkoenergetický", "ekologický") – no numbers.
7. Portfolio = gallery of renders without brief or result.
8. Catalogue of type houses with filters.
9. Contact form name / e-mail / message, map at the bottom.
10. "Viac ako X rokov skúseností", logos of partners.

Already inverted on `main`: 4 (prices + configurator), 5 (OSO number + register link). The creative branch may invert 1 and 6 harder.
